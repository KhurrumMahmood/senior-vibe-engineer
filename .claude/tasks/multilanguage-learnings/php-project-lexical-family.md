## Interface-depth and deletion evidence

Two designs were compared: literal standalone copies of the same role/tool/
Composer/lint/token/lifecycle code in five consumers, and one PHP-local fact
producer plus five final-artifact consumers. The shared form passes the rubric:

- **Deletion test:** deleting the helper forces five consumers to duplicate
  role ordering, PHP/Composer probes, native commands, token parsing, manifests,
  stale clearing, and preservation checks.
- **Caller knowledge removed:** consumers provide root, target, output, and
  configured tools; they do not know role policy, probe/version parsing,
  Composer validation, lint/parse status merging, or manifest construction.
- **Test surface:** 48 focused capability cases invoke only copied public PHP
  commands and inspect final artifacts. They never call private helper
  functions.
- **Adapter reality:** five production consumers use the same snapshot now;
  their variation begins at consumer-owned artifact interpretation.
- **Fallback:** if any later consumer needs incompatible Composer/tool/role
  policy, keep that consumer local. If the conjunctive measurements below
  regress after root publication, mechanically inline this exact helper into
  each consumer and delete `_php-project-lexical`; do not preserve a failed
  seam for architectural symmetry.

## Per-skill limits

- `adapt-project` reports objective authored-file, Composer marker/PSR-4, and
  command facts. It does not endorse layout or infer a framework.
- `explain-code` covers direct public lexical classes, interfaces, traits,
  enums, functions, and public methods up to the existing 15-symbol budget.
  Inheritance, aliases, generated members, types, callers, contracts, and
  behavior remain unexplained.
- `find-concept-divergence` is strict glossary text evidence over authored PHP,
  not identifier or conceptual identity. Comments and strings may be review
  noise; exact glossary citations remain required.
- `find-duplication` fingerprints exact native-token-normalized named bodies of
  at least five source lines. It is a clone lead, never behavioral equivalence
  or safe consolidation.
- `find-folder-topology-drift` groups direct sibling names by `_`/`-` prefix or
  leading CamelCase token at an explicit threshold. It proves no namespace,
  autoload, ownership, import, framework, or move safety.
- Composer PSR-4 arrays, multiple roots per namespace, path repositories,
  Composer plugins/scripts, autoload execution, PHPStan/Psalm facts,
  reflection, dynamic loading, conditional definitions, extensions, and
  Laravel/Symfony behavior remain outside this family.
